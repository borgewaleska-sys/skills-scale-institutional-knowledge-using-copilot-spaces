# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads oversee quality standards and testing coordination throughout the project lifecycle. They partner with developers and product managers to ensure that deliverables meet defined quality criteria and are free of critical defects.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and testing strategies aligned with project requirements
- Coordinate testing activities, including unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Manage defect identification, prioritization, and resolution
- Review acceptance criteria for testability and completeness
- Provide quality metrics and status reporting to project leadership
- Mentor team members on testing best practices and tools

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test plans, review testing approaches, provide feedback on code quality and test coverage
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria for clarity and define quality metrics; ensure requirements are testable
- **Project Managers**: Report quality status, risks, and blockers; coordinate testing timelines within project schedules
- **Compliance Officer**: Align testing strategies with regulatory and compliance requirements when applicable

### Goals
- Ensure consistent, high quality of deliverables
- Identify and resolve defects before production release
- Reduce post-release issues and support burden
- Build team capability in quality assurance practices

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and quality gates
- Defect reports and trend analysis
- Quality metrics dashboards and status updates
- Design and code review participation

---

## Stakeholder Manager

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers coordinate with external and internal stakeholders to manage expectations, ensure alignment, and maintain transparent communication throughout the project lifecycle. They serve as a bridge between the project team and broader organizational interests.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and engage key stakeholders at project initiation
- Establish communication cadence and channels for stakeholder updates
- Gather stakeholder requirements, constraints, and feedback
- Manage expectation-setting and deliver progress transparency
- Address stakeholder concerns and escalate risks that impact success
- Coordinate feedback loops to incorporate stakeholder input into project planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Partner to develop communication plans and status reporting; coordinate stakeholder events
- **Product Managers**: Help validate product vision with stakeholders and capture feedback on priorities
- **Developers**: Facilitate technical discussions with stakeholders when needed; explain technical constraints and trade-offs
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Share quality metrics and testing progress with stakeholders; gather acceptance expectations

### Goals
- Maintain strong stakeholder alignment and satisfaction
- Reduce misaligned expectations and surprises
- Enable early identification of scope or priority conflicts
- Foster collaborative relationships across organizational boundaries

### Typical Communication
- Executive briefings and stakeholder update meetings
- Feedback summaries and impact assessments
- Communication plans and engagement calendars
- Risk and concern escalations

---

## Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance Officers ensure that projects adhere to regulatory requirements, legal standards, organizational policies, and industry best practices. They provide guidance on governance, risk, and control measures to protect the organization and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Assess compliance requirements applicable to the project scope and deliverables
- Review project plans, designs, and deliverables for compliance risks
- Provide guidance on legal, regulatory, and policy constraints
- Coordinate compliance audits and control testing
- Maintain compliance documentation and audit trails
- Escalate compliance violations or risks to leadership
- Support training and awareness on compliance requirements

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Integrate compliance requirements into project plans and timelines; support audit coordination
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Collaborate on testing compliance controls and regulatory requirements
- **Product Managers**: Review product requirements for compliance implications; advise on compliant solutions
- **Developers**: Provide technical compliance guidance; review implementation against compliance standards
- **Stakeholder Manager**: Communicate compliance requirements and restrictions to relevant stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers compliant solutions that meet regulatory and legal obligations
- Minimize compliance risks and penalties
- Build organizational confidence in project delivery
- Establish clear audit trails and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Compliance assessment reports and requirement summaries
- Audit findings and remediation plans
- Policy and regulatory updates
- Compliance checkpoints and gate reviews

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical leadership and strategic guidance to ensure that project solutions are well-designed, scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational technical standards. They review critical design decisions and ensure architectural consistency across projects.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical standards, architectural patterns, and technology stack guidance
- Review solution designs for alignment with organizational standards and best practices
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Provide guidance on scalability, performance, security, and maintainability
- Ensure consistency and integration across systems and services
- Support technology evaluation and selection decisions
- Mentor developers on architectural principles and design patterns

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on design reviews, provide technical guidance, mentor on architectural patterns
- **Project Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility, resource requirements, and schedule implications of architectural decisions
- **Product Managers**: Validate that product requirements can be met within technical constraints; advise on technical trade-offs
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Support definition of testability and performance requirements; review test strategies for coverage of architectural risks
- **Stakeholder Manager**: Communicate technical constraints and opportunities to stakeholders in business terms

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are technically sound, scalable, and maintainable
- Reduce technical debt and rework through early design review
- Build organizational technical capability and standards
- Enable knowledge sharing and consistency across teams

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and technical guidance
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technical risk assessments
- Technology evaluation and recommendation reports

---

## Cross-Functional Team Lead

### Role Summary
Cross-Functional Team Leads manage coordination between different functional areas and ensure seamless knowledge sharing, collaboration, and integration. They break down silos and foster a unified team approach to project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate collaboration between functional teams (e.g., engineering, product, design, operations)
- Coordinate dependencies and handoffs between teams
- Establish shared goals and success metrics aligned across functions
- Foster knowledge sharing and cross-team learning
- Address and resolve functional conflicts and competing priorities
- Communicate cross-functional status and progress to leadership
- Support team alignment on processes, tools, and best practices

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate with PM on scheduling and dependency management; escalate cross-functional risks
- **Developers**: Align on technical requirements from other functions; facilitate knowledge transfer
- **Product Managers**: Ensure product vision is understood across all functions; gather cross-functional input on feasibility
- **Quality Assurance Lead**: Coordinate testing efforts across functional areas; align quality standards
- **Stakeholder Manager**: Help identify and engage functional stakeholders; facilitate communication channels
- **Technical Architect**: Ensure architectural decisions are communicated and understood across teams; support technical knowledge sharing

### Goals
- Enable seamless collaboration and integration across functional boundaries
- Reduce delays from poor cross-team coordination
- Build shared ownership and accountability for project outcomes
- Accelerate knowledge sharing and organizational learning

### Typical Communication
- Cross-functional planning and alignment meetings
- Integration status reports and dependency tracking
- Knowledge-sharing forums and retrospectives
- Escalation of functional conflicts and resolution
